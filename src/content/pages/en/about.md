I earned a three-year computer science degree (BUT) at IUT Lyon 1 in 2025, then joined IMT Mines Alès, where I study software engineering until 2028. I have been an apprentice at Astrée Software since August 2024, after an internship there from April to July 2024.

Astrée Software makes an MES, the software that runs production on a shop floor. I tailor it to each customer. Starting from the standard version, I add a feature or change an existing one, in Java with Spring on the back end and Angular on the front end.

This site is proof in itself. I built it with Astro, specified it feature by feature, check it in CI and measure it with Lighthouse. The rest is in the [projects](/en/projects/).

Outside of code, I have practised karate since 2013. I hold a 2nd dan black belt and the DAF, the French federation’s instructor diploma.
